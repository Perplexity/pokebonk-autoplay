# Phase 1 report: a stronger heuristic and the account preset

2026-11-25. Mewtwo, forest, **tier 2**, Ranked, build `mwsewi0r` (0.10.26), offline. No training was run. Sections are
the specification's seven (docs/spec.md §16). Everything under "measured" was played headless and is stored in
`data/pokebonk.sqlite` under the batch labels given; "expected" is said where it is only that.

Followed by a second round on the heuristic: docs/phase-1b-report.md (plan-2).

**Gate:** the new heuristic beats random and the first heuristic on median, and at least half the runs reach Overtime.
**Met on seeds 1–30** (median 99,159 against 2,622 and 133; 16 of 30 in Overtime). On seeds it was not tuned on the
share is a little under half (11 of 30 fresh, 24 of 50 held-out). The 100,000 milestone is reached: best run 275,587.

## 1. What was implemented

- **A second heuristic, `heuristic2`** (`src/agents/heuristic2/`). The first one is unchanged and still playable
  (`heuristic`, version 3). Movement and fighting are the first heuristic's brain; what is new is the run plan and
  the answers to dialogs.
  - *Run plan* (`plan.ts`, version `plan-1`, every threshold in one object, overridable per batch): loot until the
    guardian is due; wake the guardian late (zone clock 420 s, 360 s in zones 2 and 3); keep the portal closed after
    it falls and stay for the final swarm; leave 50 s into the swarm or under 65 % HP; classic path; in Overtime fight
    near the portal and cash under 75 % HP or when the tide takes 6 % of max HP a second.
  - *Looting by the plan.* The brain only loots while at most two enemies are within 15 m, which never happens on
    tier 2: 5,000 to 40,000 Pokédollars stayed unspent. The plan now orders the walk to free and paid Poké Balls,
    berries when hurt, and the shop.
  - *Valuation from effect data* (`values.ts`): what an item or vitamin is worth is computed from the game's own
    `mods`, type bonuses and effect texts for the kit of the Pokémon and a build direction (weights per stat, in the
    plan). No list of good items is typed in. Level-ups, items, the shop and Ditto use it; junk offers are banished,
    weak ones refreshed or skipped; enemy-number effects count only with a high HP bar.
- **Account preset `unlocked-v4`** (`createEnvironment({ archive })`, `--archive=`): unlocked-v3 plus an item archive
  generated by the same valuation, in four versions: `empty` (0 items), `loose` (20), `balanced` (40), `narrow` (57:
  everything worth less than 8). The preset keeps to the account's 105 slots itself, and the environment checks that
  the game adopted the archive. unlocked-v3 is unchanged; every batch records its preset.
- **An environment defect fixed.** A dialog raised in the very step the Pokémon faints (an item dialog) stopped the
  episode with an error (found in phase 0, and it cost one episode of a trial here). The game does not wait for such a
  dialog; the environment now drops it and the run ends as it does in the game.

**Not implemented, although the brief asks for it:**

- Move archive, kit archive, type archive and Build Plan favourites.
- Challenge Seals: the plan takes none (0 kept in every run). Twelve seals add 1.2 to every Overtime round.
- Raising enemy numbers on purpose, slot machines, cages, altars, Hypno, the dojo, Bill's machine, rifts.
- Offerings chosen by the survival model: the plan has a fixed order (Manantial, Dique, Rompeolas). The cash rule
  uses the tide and the HP bar, not the model's balance of heal and damage.
- Staying deep into the final swarm, as leaderboard runs do. The build does not survive it: leaving 50 s in is what
  made the gate.

### What it means for 20 million

The best run is 275,587. A leaderboard Mewtwo holds max HP 19,650, damage ×152, special ×50 and 313 items after 96
minutes; this heuristic holds max HP 428, damage ×4, 32 items after 32 minutes, and cashes Overtime in round 4 or 5.
The distance is two orders of magnitude, and it is in the build, not in Overtime play.

### Limitations and unresolved questions

- **The gate is met on the seeds the plan was tuned on, not beyond**: 44 % of the 80 unseen-seed runs reach Overtime.
- **Three Overtime bonuses were lost by fainting** (24,514, 57,198 and 140,914), all on runs that were doing well: the
  cash rule is still too slow when the horde, not the tide, does the damage.
- **Zone 2's guardian** is the most common end: woken at 360 s it is often still up when the swarm comes.
- Pokédollars: about 11,000 spent of 30,000 to 160,000 earned. The rest has no outlet yet (slot machines, cages,
  refreshing the shop).
- The valuation's weights are a first setting. Its text rules cover the 49 items without a stat line by pattern; a
  few effects (allies, status synergies) are valued roughly.
- An archive is generated for the species the environment was created for.
- The comparison of archive versions used one plan; a different plan might prefer another version.

## 7. Recommended next stage

Before the hybrid of phase 2, one more round on the heuristic is worth more than a learner on top of this one:

1. **Guardian timing by strength**, not by the clock: wake when the build kills fast enough, so zone 2's guardian is
   down before its swarm.
2. **Challenge Seals** once that holds: twelve kept would lift every Overtime round by 1.2.
3. **Spend everything**: slot machines, cages, shop refreshes; then the remaining map services.
4. **Stay longer in the swarm as the build allows**: the leave time by HP trend and kill rate, not a fixed 50 s.
5. **Cash by the model**: heal per second against tide plus hits, with a margin.

Phase 2 as written (skills and a manager) can start from `heuristic2` as it is; the proposal it copies would simply
be weaker than it could be. The owner's call.
